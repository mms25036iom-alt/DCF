# Research and valuation methodology

The workbook and report use an original Astra layout. They are not Morgan Stanley, J.P. Morgan, or DBS research, and do not imply analyst certification, affiliation, or access to a bank's proprietary model.

## Public references reviewed

- [DBS public equity report on Morgan Stanley](https://www.dbs.com/content/article/pdf/US_clover/Morgan_Stanley.pdf): a reference for organizing a concise investment overview, business description, dated market information, financial summaries, valuation explanation, and risks. Its text, recommendations, branding, and analyst identities are not copied into generated reports.
- [Aswath Damodaran's DCF checklist, NYU Stern](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/adamodar/pdfiles/eqnotes/DCFtodolist.pdf): a reference for the sequence from financial statements and business analysis through capital costs, reinvestment, cash flows, and enterprise-to-equity valuation.
- [NYU Stern discussion of working capital in valuation](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/adamodar/New_Home_Page/valquestions/noncashwc.htm): a reference for distinguishing operating working capital from cash and interest-bearing financing balances.

These references inform structure and standard analytical methods. They are not the source of a company's financial observations or evidence for a current investment recommendation.

## Source hierarchy and timing

Company filings and official investor-relations disclosures are preferred for verification. Yahoo observations are secondary data and can be incomplete, delayed, restated, or reported on a different consolidation basis. Reports retain retrieval times and supplied fiscal-period dates. A source must match the requested listing; another company is never substituted.

Missing observations remain unavailable. A forecast uses the latest complete comparable source period, with any later partial periods retained separately. A model is not evidence that a financial statement has been audited or reconciled.

## Calculation flow

1. **Historical sources** preserve original currency observations and source references.
2. **Assumptions** own the editable forecast drivers and identify observed calibrations versus explicit assumptions.
3. **Operating forecast** calculates revenue, costs, EBIT, tax, depreciation, and capital spending.
4. **Working capital** calculates operating balances and annual investment or release. A days schedule links receivables to sales, and inventory/payables to cost of revenue. Where observations are incomplete, a disclosed revenue-linked assumption may be used instead of inventing reported balances.
5. **WACC** shows cost of equity, after-tax debt cost, capital weights, and the active rate. Risk-free rate, equity premium, beta, and borrowing costs remain visible inputs. A manual discount-rate override is identified explicitly.
6. **DCF** calculates FCFF, discounts forecast cash flows, calculates a perpetual-growth terminal value, and bridges enterprise value to equity and per-share value. WACC must exceed terminal growth. Net debt and shares retain their own dates and units.
7. **Sensitivity and summary** link to the calculation schedules. They do not replace the calculation build with static valuation snapshots.

Financial amounts may be presented in crore, lakh, million, or full currency values. This changes the display divisor, not the currency or mathematical valuation. Share counts and per-share prices are not scaled by the monetary divisor.

## Report interpretation

The report distinguishes observed facts, mathematical results, and assumed future outcomes. Any AI-authored assessment is labeled separately. Confirmed catalysts, governance conclusions, forecasts, and buy/sell ratings are not invented when evidence is missing. An operating-company FCFF model is not applied to banks and insurers without a suitable financial-sector valuation method.

Public-source links exclude credential-bearing locations, local filesystem paths, and query strings or fragments that might contain private tokens. The PDF renderer does not fetch remote images or execute source HTML. Exported documents remain the owner's responsibility to share appropriately.
